1 Windows "Never Sleep" Power Setup (Every Click)

An algorithmic bot executes high-frequency calculations and manages active Stop-Loss/Take-Profit orders in real time. If Windows goes to sleep, your network connection pauses and the bot cannot monitor price action. Follow these clicks to prevent sleep:

Method A: Windows 11 & Windows 10 Settings

  1. Press Win + I to open Windows Settings.
  2. Click System → Power & battery (or Power & sleep).
  3. Under Screen and sleep:
    • When plugged in, turn off my screen after: Set to 10 or 15 minutes (screen can turn off to save power).
    • When plugged in, put my device to sleep after: Set strictly to NEVER.

Method B: Classic Control Panel (Recommended for 24/7 Laptops & Desktops)

  1. Press Win + R, type powercfg.cpl, and press Enter.
  2. Select High Performance or Balanced, and click Change plan settings.
  3. Set Put the computer to sleep: to Never.
  4. If using a laptop, click "Choose what closing the lid does" on the left sidebar, and set When I close the lid to Do nothing so the bot trades with the lid closed!

7 Troubleshooting FAQ (Common Fixes)

Q: MetaTrader 5 says "Error 10026: Trade is disabled" or "Algo Trading is disabled"?

Fix: Look at the top toolbar in MetaTrader 5 and click the "Algo Trading" button so the icon turns GREEN (Play triangle). If using Pocket Option, switch to a real CFD broker demo (MetaQuotes-Demo or Fusion Markets Demo) because binary brokers disable MetaTrader's trade API.

Q: How do I enable Google Gemini AI Veto?

Fix: Get a free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. Double-click 5_CONFIGURE_AI_MODE.bat, select option [1], and paste your key. It saves to .env automatically with hot-reload!

Q: Can I use Google Antigravity (agy.exe) for free AI audits?

Fix: Yes! If you have Antigravity installed and logged in, PocketBot Apex automatically discovers agy.exe and routes adversarial risk audits through your Google AI Pro session at $0 API cost.

Q: What if I have NO internet or NO AI keys?

Fix: PocketBot Apex seamlessly operates in Pure Math Mode (0ms, $0 token cost). 100% of real-time trading logic relies on mathematical regime filtering, Level 2 order-book math, and 10,000-path Monte Carlo simulations.

Q: How do I close all open trades immediately in an emergency?

Fix: If you configured Telegram remote control, message /panic_flatten to your Telegram bot. Alternatively, in MetaTrader 5, press Ctrl + T to open the Trade tab, right-click, and select "Close All Positions".
